## Why the note beats the article

A note is cheap to publish, which means it gets published. An article is
expensive, which means it sits in drafts for eleven months and then disappears
during a laptop migration.

- A note can be wrong in public and corrected later.
- Notes accumulate into articles; articles rarely decompose into notes.
- A dated note is an honest record of what you thought at the time.

## The shape

1. The claim.
2. The evidence, or the experience standing in for evidence.
3. The counter-argument you find most persuasive.
4. What would change your mind.
